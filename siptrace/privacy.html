SipTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

SipTrace is a personal Android app for logging alcohol and comparing it with your sleep and recovery. It is built to keep your information on your phone. This policy explains what the app uses, where it goes and how to remove it.

What SipTrace stores

  • Your drink log: the drinks you enter (type, volume, strength and time) and the days you mark as alcohol-free.
  • Settings: your theme, the data sources you choose, and the date you connected Health Connect.
  • WHOOP recovery data, if you connect WHOOP: nightly heart rate variability, resting heart rate, recovery score and sleep times.
  • WHOOP sign-in tokens, if you connect WHOOP, stored encrypted on the phone so the app can keep reading your data.

All of this is stored only on your phone. SipTrace has no server and no accounts, and the developer never receives a copy.

WHOOP

If you choose to connect WHOOP, you sign in on WHOOP's own page and approve access. SipTrace then reads your recovery, sleep and cycle data from the WHOOP API, directly from your phone. It only reads; it never changes or adds anything in your WHOOP account. WHOOP's handling of your data is covered by WHOOP's privacy policy.

Health Connect

If you allow it, SipTrace reads sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and respiratory rate from Android Health Connect, only while the app is open. It never writes to Health Connect. These readings are used on the phone to show your insights and are not saved by SipTrace.

What SipTrace does not do

  • It does not send your data to the developer or to any third party. The only network connection is to WHOOP, if you connect it.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not back up your drink log or health data to the cloud.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect WHOOP in SipTrace's settings to delete the stored tokens and WHOOP data from the phone. You can also remove SipTrace's access from your WHOOP account.
  • Health Connect access can be withdrawn at any time in Health Connect's settings.
  • Uninstalling SipTrace deletes everything it stored.

Not medical advice

SipTrace shows patterns in your own records. It does not diagnose or treat anything and is not medical advice.
